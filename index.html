The projection optics are treated as one equivalent lens. The LDT files with Ø30 and Ø40 gobos give the focal length and light field; on-site measurements at 0.75–5.5 m give the visible-edge scale and focus scale. The visible edge is taken at about 37% of the local field. The aperture is set from site observation (at most about 10 cm of blur per metre of throw). Stray light outside the edge, about 5% of flux in the LDT, is not shown. Shutters are assumed to lie in the gobo plane.

About this tool

This is my private initiative to make life easier, based on daily experience from more than 20 years in the lighting industry.

Set the distance, the angles and the lens position, and see straight away how a framing or gobo projector lands on a wall or the ground: spot size, keystone distortion, sharpness and illuminance. It shows direct light only, computed per pixel from the fixture's photometry and calibrated optics.

Everything runs in your browser. Your gobo images are never uploaded anywhere.
