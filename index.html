How to read it
The visible edge is the ≈37% level of the field, where the eye places the border of the spot; sizes are measured to it. Illuminance comes from the LDT with distance and angle of incidence. Blur grows where the wall leaves the plane of focus. Next: a pre-distorted gobo for ordering, loading any LDT/IES, and stray light outside the edge.

About this tool

This is my private initiative to make life easier, based on daily experience from more than 20 years in the lighting industry.

Set the distance to the wall, the horizontal and vertical angles and the lens position, and see straight away how a framing projector or gobo projector lands on the wall: spot size, keystone distortion, sharpness and illuminance. It shows direct light only, computed per pixel from the fixture's photometry and calibrated optics.

Everything runs in your browser. Your gobo images are never uploaded anywhere.

How the fixture is modelled

The projection optics are treated as one equivalent lens. For the iGuzzini Palco Framer S892 the LDT files with Ø30 and Ø40 gobos give the effective focal length (90.6 mm at the 50% edge) and the light field. On-site measurements at 0.75–5.5 m give the visible-edge scale (87.3 mm, Ø = 0.342·L) and the focus scale x = 11.0 + 2.59/(L − 0.50) mm.

  • The visible edge is taken at ≈37% of the local field level.
  • Lens aperture for depth of field (19 mm) is set from on-site observation: at most about 10 cm of edge blur per metre of throw across the full lens travel.
  • Stray light outside the edge (about 5% of flux in the LDT) is not shown.
  • Shutters are assumed to lie in the gobo plane; open gate Ø40 mm.
